Hospital discharge follow-up, automated

When someone leaves the hospital or the ER, a nurse care manager should check in with them within a few days. Missed check-ins are a common reason people end up back in the hospital. This project spots each discharge, scores how risky it is, and creates a follow-up case in Salesforce for the right nurse. The case includes deadlines and an AI-drafted call plan.

The AI never contacts patients. It only drafts; a nurse reviews and approves every case before anyone is called. Risk scores and deadlines come from explicit rules, not AI.

How it works

  1. Detect a finished hospital stay or ER visit in the health record (FHIR).
  2. Score risk with the LACE readmission index (hospital stays) or ER rules.
  3. Route it to the right kind of follow-up.
  4. Set deadlines in business days (weekends and holidays skipped).
  5. Draft a call plan with a local AI model. Only clinical facts are sent to it, never names or contact details.
  6. Create the Salesforce case, at most once per discharge. A Salesforce Flow assigns it to the nurse team.
